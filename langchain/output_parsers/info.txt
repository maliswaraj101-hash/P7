suppose we want to make a system where we send the LLM a topic and then we get a detailed report of it.
after getting the report , we send the report again to get the summary in 5 lines.

topic --> LLM --> detailed report --> LLM --> 5 lines of summary


without parser we would go this by doing , 
1-> prompting LLM then getting result.content ,
2-> set another template for 5 lines of summary and then get result.content


with parser we can simply make chains

chain = template1 | model | parser | template2 | model | parser
